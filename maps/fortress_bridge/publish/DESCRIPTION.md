# 长桥要塞 · Fortress Bridge

---

## 中文

**两边各守一岸，中间只有一座桥。** 地图像一个 H：两队分在左右两条竖边上，中间的一横是一座又长又窄的陆桥，两侧全是深水。想打过去，只能从桥上走。

### 地图

| | |
|---|---|
| 阵营 | 第一位玩家所在的队伍在左岸，其他队伍在右岸 |
| 通道 | 中间一座陆桥，约 48 米宽，两侧是深水，没有别的路；停在一侧的战舰能打到大半座桥 |
| 水域 | 上下都是深湖，走不过去；两岸都有浅滩，可以捕鱼、造码头 |
| 资源 | 标准开局资源，两岸后方各多一座金矿和石矿 |

### 怎么玩

- 推荐配合游戏模式 **《堡垒模式》**：真人一队、AI 一队，AI 会一波波从桥上冲过来。
- 也可以当普通的团队对战图，两边对称。
- 桥头是天然的防线：在桥头修墙、放投石机，看着对面挤上桥。
- 所有真人放在同一队，AI 放在另一队。

## English

**One shore each, and only a bridge between.** The map is an H: the two teams start on its two uprights, and its crossbar is a long, narrow land bridge with deep water on both sides. The only way over is across the bridge.

### The map

| | |
|---|---|
| Sides | The first player's team takes the left shore; every other team the right |
| Way across | One land bridge in the middle, about 48 m wide, with deep water on both sides and no other way over; a warship on one side covers most of it |
| Water | Deep lakes above and below: no wading, but both shores have shallows for fishing and docks |
| Resources | The standard start, plus an extra gold and stone deposit at the back of each shore |

### How to play

- Made for the **Fortress Mode** game mode: humans on one team, AIs on the other, sending wave after wave across the bridge.
- Also works as a plain, symmetric team game.
- The bridgehead is a natural front line: wall it, put trebuchets behind it, and watch the other side crowd onto the bridge.
- Put all humans on one team and all AIs on the other.
