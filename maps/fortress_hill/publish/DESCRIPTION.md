# 山顶要塞 · Fortress Hill

---

## 中文

**大家一起守中央高地。** 守方的出生点聚在地图中央一块不高的高地上，四周是悬崖；每位守方带来一对相邻的坡道，所以每人都有两个挨得很近的口子要守。攻方散布在地图四周，从各个方向往坡道冲。

### 地图

| | |
|---|---|
| 守方 | 人数较少的队伍，出生点都在高地中央，1～4 人都行 |
| 攻方 | 另一队，均匀分布在地图四周 |
| 通道 | 每位守方一对相邻的坡道，按左、右、上、下依次分配 |
| 地势 | 高地只比平地高一点，看着自然，远程单位略占优势 |
| 资源 | 标准开局资源，高地四角各多一座金矿或石矿 |

### 怎么玩

- 推荐配合游戏模式 **《堡垒模式》**：真人 + 若干 AI，AI 会一波波攻城。
- 也可以当普通的团队对战图：人少的一方守，人多的一方攻。
- 2 人守的话，坡道在左右两侧，一人守一边、背靠背。
- 所有真人放在同一队，AI 放在另一队。

## English

**Hold the hill together.** The defenders start together on a low plateau in the middle of the map, ringed by cliffs. Each defender brings a pair of ramps close to each other, so every player has two nearby gaps to hold. The attackers are spread around the edge of the map and storm the ramps from every side.

### The map

| | |
|---|---|
| Defenders | The smaller team, starting together in the middle; one to four players |
| Attackers | The other team, spread evenly around the edge |
| Ways in | A pair of close ramps per defender, placed left, right, top and bottom in turn |
| Height | The plateau is only a little higher than the plain: natural to look at, a small edge for ranged units |
| Resources | The standard start, plus an extra gold or stone deposit in each corner of the plateau |

### How to play

- Made for the **Fortress Mode** game mode: humans against AIs that send siege waves.
- Also works as a plain team game: the smaller team defends, the larger one attacks.
- With two defenders the ramps are on the left and right, so you hold one side each, back to back.
- Put all humans on one team and all AIs on the other.
