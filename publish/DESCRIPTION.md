# 堡垒模式 · Fortress Mode

---

## 中文

**和朋友一起守城打 AI。** 敌方 AI 轮流派出攻城波次，还会定期全军总攻；你的单位和城墙则耐打得多。适合想合作守城、看着 AI 一波波撞上城墙的玩家。

这是一个**游戏模式**：在自定义房间选择“堡垒模式”即可。标准模式的胜利条件、初始资源、时代、迷雾等选项全部保留，堡垒设置是额外加的一组选项。

### 功能

| 功能 | 默认 | 可选 |
|---|---|---|
| 玩家单位血量 | ×2 | ×1 ~ ×5 |
| 玩家防御建筑血量（城墙、城门、箭塔、前哨、城堡） | ×4 | ×1 ~ ×10 |
| 禁用巨型投石机 | 所有人 | 关 / 仅 AI / 仅玩家 / 所有人 |
| 玩家人口上限 | 300 | 200 ~ 500 |
| AI 人口上限 | 200 | 100 ~ 300 |
| AI 造兵速度 | ×2 | ×1 ~ ×3 |
| 金矿、石矿储量 | ×2 | ×1 ~ ×5 |
| 开局村民 | 16 | 6 ~ 30 |
| 攻城波次规模 | 100 人 | 关 / 50 ~ 200 |
| 首波时间 | 10 分钟 | 1（测试）~ 20 分钟 |
| 波次间隔 | 1 分钟 | 1 ~ 15 分钟 |
| AI 定期总攻（第 8 分钟起） | 每 2 分钟 | 关 / 1 ~ 10 分钟 |
| 存活胜利 | 60 分钟 | 关 / 5（测试）~ 120 分钟 |

### 攻城波次怎么运作

- 敌方 AI 轮流出兵，从各自的**主城镇中心**旁出发，每波随机选一位真人玩家作为目标。
- 步兵是武士、长矛兵和弓箭手，随 AI 的时代升级。
- 攻城器械不占步兵名额：首波 2 辆冲车 + 1 门手推炮，之后每波各多 1，最多 18 车 + 10 炮。
- 每第 5 波是**精英波**：骑士、武士、手炮兵、弩手，全是四本满级兵，不管 AI 在几本。
- 上一波活下来的兵会和新一波一起再冲一次。残兵太多时，新一波会相应减少，场上不会无限堆兵。
- 攻破一家 AI 的主城镇中心，它就再也派不出增援。
- AI 定期总攻：第 8 分钟开始，每隔一段时间，随机一家主城镇中心还在的 AI 派出全部陆军。

### 其他

- AI 村民上限 100，多出的人口用来造兵。
- 存活胜利：坚持到设定时间，真人一方获胜；征服、奇观、圣地等标准胜利方式照常有效。
- 可以和 AI 类的 Tuning Pack 一起用。
- 适合真人对 AI 的合作局，所有真人在同一队效果最好。

### 提示

- 想快速体验，首波选 1 分钟、存活胜利选 5 分钟。
- 觉得卡，就把波次规模调小、波次间隔调长，或者关掉定期总攻。
- 觉得太难，先把 AI 造兵速度和总攻频率调低。

## English

**Co-op against the AI behind tough walls.** Enemy AIs take turns sending siege waves and launch regular all-out attacks, while your units and walls are far sturdier. Made for players who want to hold a fortress together and watch the AI crash into it, wave after wave.

This is a **game mode**: pick "Fortress Mode" in a custom lobby. Every standard option is kept — win conditions, starting resources, starting age, fog of war and the rest — and the fortress settings are an extra group of options.

### Features

| Feature | Default | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Human unit health | ×2 | ×1 – ×5 |
| Human defense health (walls, gates, towers, outposts, keeps) | ×4 | ×1 – ×10 |
| Disable trebuchets | Everyone | Off / AI only / Humans only / Everyone |
| Human population cap | 300 | 200 – 500 |
| AI population cap | 200 | 100 – 300 |
| AI unit production speed | ×2 | ×1 – ×3 |
| Gold and stone deposits | ×2 | ×1 – ×5 |
| Starting villagers | 16 | 6 – 30 |
| Attack wave size | 100 units | Off / 50 – 200 |
| First wave | 10 min | 1 (test) – 20 min |
| Wave interval | 1 min | 1 – 15 min |
| AI all-out attack (from minute 8) | Every 2 min | Off / 1 – 10 min |
| Survival victory | 60 min | Off / 5 (test) – 120 min |

### How the waves work

- Enemy AIs take turns. Each wave leaves from the AI's **main town center** and targets a random human player.
- Infantry are men-at-arms, spearmen and archers, upgraded with the AI's age.
- Siege comes on top of the infantry: 2 rams and 1 bombard in the first wave, one more of each every wave, up to 18 rams and 10 bombards.
- Every 5th wave is an **elite wave**: knights, men-at-arms, handcannoneers and crossbowmen, all Age IV, whatever the AI's age.
- Survivors of earlier waves charge again together with the new one. When too many survive, the next wave is smaller, so units never pile up without limit.
- Destroy an AI's main town center and it can send no more waves.
- AI all-out attack: from minute 8, every few minutes one AI whose main town center still stands sends its whole land army.

### Also

- AI villagers are capped at 100, so the rest of their population goes into the army.
- Survival victory: hold out until the set time and the human side wins; conquest, wonder, sacred sites and the other standard victories still apply.
- Works together with AI tuning packs.
- Made for humans-versus-AI co-op; it plays best with all humans on one team.

### Tips

- For a quick look, set the first wave to 1 minute and survival victory to 5 minutes.
- If the game slows down, use smaller or less frequent waves, or turn off the all-out attacks.
- If it is too hard, lower the AI production speed and the all-out attack frequency first.
